UGWAD: Heritage Woven by Hand

Supporting the traditional smoked buri weavers of Barangay San Francisco, Anini-y, Antique. Discover authentic handwoven banig, gift bags, and decorative panels crafted to sustain local livelihoods.

Shop Handwoven Goods Learn to Weave

100% Handwoven Buri

Every piece is harvested, stripped, smoked, and woven by hand using natural buri palm leaves from Anini-y, Antique.

Community-Led Since 2018

Founded in 2018, UGWAD (Kinaray-a for "to progress") empowers local women weavers through fair, direct-to-buyer orders.

Preserving Cultural Heritage

Each purchase keeps the traditional mat weaving craft alive for the next generation of Antiqueno artisans.
