Our Story: Weaving Progress in Anini-y

Established in 2018 in Barangay San Francisco, Anini-y, Antique, the UGWAD weavers association unites local artisans to turn traditional smoked buri weaving into a sustainable community enterprise and livelihood

What "UGWAD" Means

In Kinaray-a and Hiligaynon, "ugwad" means to progress, grow, or prosper. The group chose this name to reflect their goal of giving livelihood and uplifting weaving families in Anini-y, Antique.

Local Weaving Terms

  • Buri: Native palm species whose dried, smoked leaves provide durable natural fibers.
  • Banig: Traditional handwoven sleeping or decorative mat.
  • Lala: The intricate over-and-under handweaving technique passed down across generations.
