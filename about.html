Our Story & Mission

UGWAD (Hiligaynon for progress or growth) is a digital showcase and ordering platform dedicated to sustaining the traditional Patadyong and Hablon weaving industry in Iloilo.

Why We Built UGWAD

Local weavers face declining youth interest and limited market reach. UGWAD bridges this gap by sharing the cultural story behind every weave and providing an accessible online catalog where customers can order directly.

Meet the Weaving Community

Our partner weavers in Miagao and neighboring towns craft each piece using time-honored techniques passed down through generations.
